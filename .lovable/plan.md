Use the strongest patterns from the portfolio article without copying its article layout: lead with the strongest insight, respect the reader's time, show process through real artifacts, and make Sebastian's contribution unmistakable.

- Preserve all factual content, but shorten repeated passages and break dense text into clearer reading beats.
- Add a compact case overview near the top: problem, contribution, outcome, and limitation.
- Add a simple chapter index linking to the five sections so readers can scan or jump directly.
- Give each section one prominent takeaway before supporting detail.
- Pair the existing rescue photo and process sketches with the sections they prove, rather than leaving imagery as decoration.
- Make “Reading it now” the strongest final chapter, clearly separating the assumption, today’s test, and the kill-line.
- Keep the existing header, footer, type system, CTA, and honest wording. Add no metrics, testimonials, or claims.
